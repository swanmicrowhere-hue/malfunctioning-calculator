Privacy Policy

Malfunctioning Calculator

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Malfunctioning Calculator (the “App”) handles information when you use it. For privacy-related questions, contact swanmicrowhere@gmail.com.

1. Calculator features and local data

The App provides calculator modes, visual effects, and an Easter egg achievement journal. The App is designed to save preferences and discovered achievements locally on your device. These local records are not intended to be transmitted to the developer. Removing the App or clearing its data may erase them.

2. Advertising and third-party services

The App uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device or advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and other data in connection with ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and, where permitted, personalization. The exact data processed depends on SDK behavior, device settings, applicable law, and your consent choices.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure guidance.

3. Consent and privacy choices

Where required, the App requests consent or presents privacy choices for advertising using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Your choices may affect whether ads are personalized. You may also manage certain advertising preferences through your device or Google account settings. Where legally required, a way to revisit applicable privacy choices should be available within the App.

4. How information is used

Local app preferences and achievements support the App's functionality. Advertising-related information processed by Google and its partners may be used to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising, subject to applicable requirements and choices.

5. Sharing and retention

The developer does not intend to sell locally stored calculator preferences or achievement records. Advertising data may be processed or shared by Google and its advertising partners under their own policies. Locally stored app data generally remains on your device until you clear it or uninstall the App. Google and its partners determine retention of information they process under their policies.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not specifically designed for children. Advertising configuration and age-related protections are subject to the App's final Google Play target-audience declarations and applicable law.

7. Security

We aim to limit collection by keeping calculator settings and achievement records on the device. No internet-connected service can be guaranteed completely secure, and third-party advertising services operate under their own security practices.

8. Your rights and requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding personal data, including access, deletion, or objection to certain processing. For questions about the developer's handling of information, contact swanmicrowhere@gmail.com. For data controlled by Google, consult Google's privacy controls and policy.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App's features, advertising setup, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this page with an updated date.
